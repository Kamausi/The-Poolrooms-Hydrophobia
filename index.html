The park is still running. The pumps work, the towels are warm, the announcements play. Nobody is here. The guest counter changes when you are alone.

Crew Seven went down six weeks ago. Their raft came back up the slide empty, four life vests folded on the seat. Ride the slide. Shoot what moves in the water. Get off at the docks, find the power, and reach the Safe Rooms before the chlorine, or the calm, gets to you.
